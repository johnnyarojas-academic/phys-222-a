* List of Equations for Homework
** Electric Force
The electric force is defined mathematically as:

\begin{align*}
F_{\text{electric}} = q \vec{E}
\end{align*}

** Magnetic Force
The magnetic force is defined mathematically as:

\begin{align*}
F_{\text{magnetic}} = q \vec{v} \times \vec{B}
\end{align*}

** The Lorentz Force
The loretnz force equation unites the electric and magnetic interactions into a single mathematical definition:

\begin{align*}
\vec{F} = q(\vec{E} + v \times \vec{E}) \quad \text{or} \quad \vec{F} = \vec{E}q + qv \times \vec{b} \quad \text{and also} \quad F = F_{\text{electric}} + F_{\text{magnetic}}
\end{align*}
