#+title: Solonoids

* Formulas for the Magntic Field in a Solonoid
** Exact
The full formula for the magnetic field due to a sonoloid accounting for the complete geometry
\begin{align*}
\Delta B_z = \frac{\mu_0}{4\pi} \frac{ 2\pi R^2 I }{ ((d - z)^2 + R^2)^{\frac{3}{2}} } \frac{N}{L}\Delta z
\end{align*}
** Aproximation
This approximation doesnt take into account the edges of the coil
\begin{align*}
B_z \approx \frac{\mu_0 N I}{L}
\end{align*}

* Magnetic Dipole Moment
\begin{align*}
B_{axis} = \frac{\mu_0}{4\pi} \frac{2\mu}{r^3}
\end{align*}
where:
- $\mu = IA$
