* 23.2 Fields Traveling Through Space

** Acceleration and Electric Fields, Simple Explanation
The electric field is dependent on the acceleration of the charges, specifically the electric field at a point is related to the distance from the point to the charge and the perpendicular acceleration of the charge to that radius.

* The Poynting Vector tells us about intensity

\begin{equation}
\vec{S} = \frac{1}{\mu_0} \vec{E} \times \vec{B}
\end{equation}
The magnitude of S tells us the power/area (intensity)

\begin{equation}
|\vec{S}|_{avg} = I
\end{equation}

Where $I$ is intensity.

*** Example
Suppose that:
$\vec{S}_{avg}$ is 900 W/m^2,
A = 0.75 m^2
